# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Photographers on a Mac who edit in Lightroom, mostly the cloud version (Lightroom CC), some in Lightroom Classic or
Camera Raw. They own presets, often hundreds from purchased packs, and want a preset's look on only part of a photo:
behind the subject, on the sky, on skin. Primary users are the owner and the photographers they share the app with.

## Product Purpose

Partly, a preset to mask converter for Lightroom, turns presets the photographer already owns into new presets that apply the same look only inside a
Lightroom AI mask. Success is going from "I want this look only on the background" to a set of new presets imported
into Lightroom in a minute or two, while trusting what carried over.

## Positioning

It reads the presets already on the Mac (it finds Lightroom's synced preset library on its own) and rebuilds each look
inside the exact mask encoding Lightroom writes for its own Select Background, Subject, Sky, People and Landscape
masks, 24 places in all. Before anything is created it shows, setting by setting, what carries over exactly,
approximately, to the whole photo, or not at all.

## Operating Context

- Used next to Lightroom on a Mac, in occasional batch sessions: pick presets, pick masks, create, import once.
- Import into Lightroom (cloud) is File › Import Profiles & Presets with the `.zip` the app writes; Classic and Camera
  Raw can be installed by the app directly.
- Ships mainly as a macOS app: a native window around the web interface. The same interface also runs in a browser
  (`node scripts/serve.mjs`), and there is a command-line tool.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. One Swift file plus the web interface; no dependencies.
- Entirely local: no network requests. Lightroom's own files are only ever read. The app writes only to its output
  folder and, on request, `~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CameraRaw/Settings/Partly` (1.0.0's `LR Mask Presets` folders are moved to the new names on first launch).
- 24 masks in three families (scene, people, landscape). Each setting is reported as Exact, Approximate, Whole photo
  or Not in mask.
- Accepts `.xmp`, `.lrtemplate`, `.zip` packs and folders, by picker or drag and drop.
- Not notarized by Apple. License not chosen yet. Repository is private.
- Not made by or affiliated with Adobe: never use Adobe's or Lightroom's logos or branding.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Partly (1.0.0 was called LR Mask Presets). Described as "Preset to Mask Converter for Lightroom": the words people search, with Lightroom only in the descriptor.
- The user's own words for the look, binding: "make this look like apple built the app with all kinds of blur and
  glass effects also with amazing animations".

## Evidence on Hand

- `web/samples/`: eight original sample presets made for this project (synthetic, safe to show).
- `docs/`: app icon, screenshots and a demo GIF, generated by `scripts/capture-docs.mjs`.
- No testimonials, user counts or press exist; never invent them. Never show the user's purchased presets in
  screenshots or anything committed.

## Product Principles

- Show before you change: what carries over is visible before anything is created.
- Hands off Lightroom's files; everything stays on the Mac.
- Fewest steps from a preset to a masked preset inside Lightroom.
- Built for whole libraries: hundreds of presets at once must stay fast and clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement confirmed. Default: WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, and the macOS accessibility
settings Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast are honored.
